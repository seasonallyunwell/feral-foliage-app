# Affiliate Disclosure (FeralFoliage.com)

## Short version (footer / small print)
Some links on this site may be affiliate links. If you buy through these links, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you.

## Standard version (recommended for blog posts)
Disclosure: Some links on this page are affiliate links, meaning we may earn a commission if you make a purchase through them, at no additional cost to you. We only recommend products we genuinely think are useful.

## Amazon-specific line (use when Amazon links are present)
As an Amazon Associate, we earn from qualifying purchases.

## Notes / best practices
- Put the disclosure **near the top** of any post that contains affiliate links.
- Avoid “medical” or “pest cure” claims; keep language practical and experience-based.
- If you later add non-Amazon affiliate programs, this disclosure still works.
