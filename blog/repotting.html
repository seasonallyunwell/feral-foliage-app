Pot Size Guide

The "One Size Up" Rule

Generally, go up 1-2 inches in diameter. Going too big = soil stays wet too long = root rot.

Current PotNext SizeNotes
2-4"+1"Small plants are sensitive to overwatering
4-8"+1-2"Standard upgrade
8-12"+2"Larger pots dry slower
12"++2-4"Big plants can handle bigger jumps

When NOT to size up

  • Plant is stressed or sick — repot into same size with fresh soil
  • You want to keep the plant small (bonsai-style)
  • It's a slow grower that likes being slightly root-bound (Snake plants, ZZ)

Soil Mix Recipes

🌿 Tropical Mix (Monstera, Pothos, Philodendron)

50% Potting Soil
30% Perlite
20% Bark

Well-draining but retains some moisture. Add bark for extra aeration.

🌵 Succulent/Cactus Mix

40% Potting Soil
40% Perlite
20% Sand

Fast-draining. These plants rot easily — err on the side of gritty.

🪴 Aroid Mix (Alocasia, Anthurium)

40% Potting Soil
30% Perlite
20% Bark
10% Charcoal

Chunky and airy. Aroids have sensitive roots that hate sitting in water.

🌿 Fern Mix (High Humidity)

50% Potting Soil
20% Perlite
30% Peat/Coco Coir

Moisture-retentive. Ferns like it damp but not soggy.

Repotting Tips

  • Water 1-2 days before — easier to remove from pot, less root stress
  • Tease out circling roots — they won't magically uncircle in the new pot
  • Don't bury the stem deeper — keep the soil line the same
  • Wait a few days to water after repotting — let damaged roots heal
  • Skip fertilizer for 2-4 weeks — fresh soil has nutrients, roots are recovering
