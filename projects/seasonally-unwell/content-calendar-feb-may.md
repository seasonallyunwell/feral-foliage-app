# Seasonal Content Calendar: Feb - May 2026

## Strategy
Shift from "just products" to "lifestyle/vibes." Use key dates to anchor product pushes.

## February: Anti-Valentine's & Self-Love 💔
**Vibe:** Dark romance, prioritizing yourself, sarcasm about traditional romance.
*   **Focus Products:** "Bleeding Hearts Skull," "Potion of Patience."
*   **Key Dates:**
    *   **Feb 13 (Galentine's):** Squad/Coven focus. "Tags your plotters."
    *   **Feb 14:** "In a relationship with my coffee" or "Taken by the void."

## March: Spring Equinox / Ostara 🌱
**Vibe:** Rebirth, but make it spooky. Things waking up from the dead (winter).
*   **Focus Products:** Botanical designs, "Tiki Bar Black Cat" (warming up).
*   **Key Dates:**
    *   **Mar 20 (Equinox):** Balance light and dark.
    *   **Theme:** "Spring Cleaning (Your Spirit)."

## April: Halfway to Halloween 🎃
**Vibe:** The 6-month countdown. Permission to be spooky in spring.
*   **Focus Products:** All Ghost designs ("Knit Ghost," "Ghost Heart Hands").
*   **Key Dates:**
    *   **April 30 (Walpurgis Night):** Witches' Night. High engagement opportunity.
    *   **Campaign:** "Why wait for October?"

## May: Garden Goth 🥀
**Vibe:** Poison gardens, herbs, dirt under fingernails.
*   **Focus Products:** Botanical skulls, nature-themed witchy items.
*   **Content Ideas:** "Poison Garden Essentials," "Witchy Gardening Tips."
