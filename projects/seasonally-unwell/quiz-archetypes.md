# Lead Magnet: "Which Coven Archetype Are You?" 🔮

**Goal:** Segment email subscribers by interest (Plants, Cozy, Edgy, Tech).
**Tone:** Fun, low-stakes, slightly roasting the user (with love).

---

## The Questions

**1. Pick a morning beverage:**
*   A. Herbal tea I blended myself (possibly dirt in it). [Green]
*   B. Black coffee. As dark as my soul. [Chaos]
*   C. Oat milk latte with 4 shots of espresso. [Tech]
*   D. Hot chocolate with marshmallows (I am healing my inner child). [Kitchen/Cozy]

**2. Your ideal Friday night looks like:**
*   A. Repotting plants and talking to them. [Green]
*   B. A dive bar, loud music, bad decisions. [Chaos]
*   C. Coding/Gaming until 3am with blue light glasses on. [Tech]
*   D. Baking bread and watching a documentary about cults. [Kitchen/Cozy]

**3. Pick a familiar:**
*   A. A toad named Bartholomew. [Green]
*   B. A raccoon (he screams). [Chaos]
*   C. A robotic cat or just my laptop. [Tech]
*   D. A very fluffy cat that sleeps 23 hours a day. [Kitchen/Cozy]

**4. What's your toxic trait?**
*   A. I bring home rocks/sticks and forget to wash them. [Green]
*   B. I start projects at midnight and abandon them at 1am. [Chaos]
*   C. I have 400 tabs open and I need them all. [Tech]
*   D. I aggressively bake for people who didn't ask. [Kitchen/Cozy]
