# .com Content Hub Concept

**Goal:** Differentiate `.com` (Brand/Lifestyle) from `.shop` (Storefront). Increase SEO and dwell time.

## 1. The "Grimoire" (Blog) 📖
Don't call it a blog. Call it the Grimoire or "The Archives."
*   **Category: Spooky History:** Short, fun histories of superstitions (e.g., "Why *do* we knock on wood?").
*   **Category: Style Guides:** "How to style a graphic tee for the office (without scaring HR)."
*   **Category: Behind the Design:** Show the sketch-to-shirt process.

## 2. Interactive Lead Magnet 🧲
**"Which Coven Archetype Are You?" Quiz**
*   *The Green Witch* (Plants, nature)
*   *The Kitchen Witch* (Food, home)
*   *The Tech Witch* (Modern, digital)
*   *The Chaos Witch* (Random, energetic)
*   **Result:** Gives them a "profile" and recommends specific shirt designs based on their type. captures email.

## 3. Lookbook / Coven Gallery 📸
*   Aggregated User Generated Content (UGC).
*   "Spotted in the Wild" section.
*   Links directly to products.
