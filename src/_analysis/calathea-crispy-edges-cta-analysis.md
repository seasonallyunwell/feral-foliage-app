## Key Insights

1. **Natural integration:** The blog post already mentions humidifiers and TDS meters organically - affiliate CTAs will feel like helpful recommendations, not sales pitches.

2. **High intent readers:** Readers seeking help with crispy edges are actively problem-solving and likely to purchase solutions.

3. **Two distinct buyer journeys:** 
   - Humidity path: Users who realize humidity is their issue
   - Water quality path: Users who want to test their water before committing to filtered water long-term

4. **Seasonal opportunity:** Prevention Protocol mentions "Winter heating dries out air" - great context for humidifier CTA in late fall.
