## The Champions

### 1. Snake Plant (Sansevieria)

**Light:** Any (low to bright)
**Water:** Every 2-4 weeks
**Why it's unkillable:** Stores water in leaves. Tolerates neglect, low light, irregular watering. The tank of houseplants.

**Care tip:** Less is more. Don't water until soil is completely dry.

---

### 2. ZZ Plant (Zamioculcas zamiifolia)

**Light:** Very low to bright
**Water:** Every 3-4 weeks
**Why it's unkillable:** Stores water in rhizomes underground. Can survive months without water. Thrives on neglect.

**Care tip:** It's fine in low light. It's fine in bright light. It doesn't care.

---

### 3. Pothos (Epipremnum aureum)

**Light:** Low to bright indirect
**Water:** Every 1-2 weeks
**Why it's unkillable:** Tells you when it needs water by drooping, forgives immediately after watering. Grows fast, easy to propagate.

**Care tip:** Let it droop slightly, then water. It will forgive you.

---

### 4. Spider Plant (Chlorophytum comosum)

**Light:** Bright indirect (tolerates low)
**Water:** Every 1-2 weeks
**Why it's unkillable:** Produces babies (pups) you can propagate. Forgiving of watering mistakes. Hardy as hell.

**Care tip:** Easy to propagate from babies. Share with friends.

---

### 5. Aloe Vera

**Light:** Bright to full sun
**Water:** Every 2-3 weeks
**Why it's unkillable:** Succulent that stores water in leaves. Thrives on neglect. Useful for burns.

**Care tip:** Let soil dry completely between waterings. More drought than drown.

---

### 6. Cast Iron Plant (Aspidistra)

**Light:** Low to medium
**Water:** Every 2-3 weeks
**Why it's unkillable:** Named for its toughness. Tolerates low light, neglect, temperature changes.

**Care tip:** It doesn't need much. Ignore it and it will be fine.

---

### 7. Jade Plant (Crassula ovata)

**Light:** Bright direct or indirect
**Water:** Every 2-3 weeks
**Why it's unkillable:** Succulent that grows slowly and lives forever. Tolerates drought.

**Care tip:** Let leaves get slightly wrinkled before watering. It will plump back up.

---

### 8. Ponytail Palm (Beaucarnea recurvata)

**Light:** Bright indirect
**Water:** Every 2-3 weeks
**Why it's unkillable:** Stores water in trunk. Drought-tolerant. Architectural look.

**Care tip:** Trunk gets squishy if overwatered. When in doubt, don't water.

---

### 9. Chinese Evergreen (Aglaonema)

**Light:** Low to medium
**Water:** Every 1-2 weeks
**Why it's unkillable:** Survives low light and inconsistent watering. Pretty silver-green leaves.

**Care tip:** Let top inch of soil dry between waterings.

---

### 10. Prayer Plant (Maranta leuconeura)

**Light:** Low to medium
**Water:** Keep slightly moist
**Why it's unkillable:** Dramatic but forgiving. Tells you when it needs water (leaves curl).

**Care tip:** If it curls, water. If it droops, water. It will forgive you.

## The Secret to Success

The secret to keeping these plants alive?

**Ignore them.**

They don't need your constant attention. They don't need daily checks. They need you to forget about them and water when you remember.

**Set calendar reminders** for once a month.
**Check soil** before watering.
**Water** when needed.
**Leave it alone.**

That's it.
