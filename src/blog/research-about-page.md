# Research: Feral Foliage About Page Strategy

<p style="margin:0 0 16px 0;color:#a7a7a7">Analysis of competitor About pages, E-E-A-T requirements, and FF brand alignment for creating an effective About page.</p>

## Executive Summary

This research document analyzes competitor About pages from major plant brands (Patch Plants, OurHousePlants, The Sill, and Bloomscape), identifies E-E-A-T requirements for SEO success, reviews the established Feral Foliage brand voice, and provides actionable recommendations for crafting an About page that differentiates FF while building trust and authority.

---

## 1. Competitor Analysis

### 1.1 Patch Plants (UK Market Leader)

**Structure:**
- Founded story: London 2016, first online plant brand in UK
- Mission statement: "make plants easy," demystify plants
- Quality assurance: Royal FloraHolland A1 badge, 40+ growers
- Trust signals: Good Housekeeping Institute Best Plant Delivery 2022-2024
- Scale metrics: 2M plants delivered, 500K customers
- Sustainability: Net-zero plant delivery retailer goal
- Community: Plant doctors, customer service, Instagram presence

**E-E-A-T Elements:**
- Experience: First-mover advantage in online plant retail
- Expertise: Plant doctors, quality checks at every stage
- Authoritativeness: Industry recognition, massive scale
- Trust: 30-day happiness guarantee, customer service hours listed

**Key Takeaway:** Patch leads with authority and scale while keeping messaging approachable ("Big Ken the kentia palm" vs. Latin names).

---

### 1.2 OurHousePlants (Informal Authority)

**Structure:**
- Team experience claim: "~50 years of hands-on experience"
- Target audience: Beginner to experienced indoor gardeners
- Value proposition: Help users become houseplant experts

**E-E-A-T Elements:**
- Experience: Collective decades of horticulture skills
- Expertise: Hands-on, practical experience focus
- Authoritativeness: Implicit through experience claim

**Key Takeaway:** Simple but effective—leads with credible experience claim without overwhelming details. Works for a content-focused site.

### 1.4 Bloomscape (Family Heritage)

**Structure:**

## 2. E-E-A-T Requirements for About Pages

### 2.1 What Google Looks For

Google's Quality Rater Guidelines emphasize these E-E-A-T factors for About pages:

1. **Experience** - First-hand experience with subject matter
2. **Expertise** - Knowledge, skills, qualifications
3. **Authoritativeness** - Recognized authority in field, citations, reviews
4. **Trustworthiness** - Transparency, contact info, honest business practices

### 2.2 E-E-A-T Checklist for Feral Foliage

Based on competitor analysis and SEO best practices, the FF About page should include:

- [ ] **Founder/author story** - Who created FF and why (experience)
- [ ] **Credentials or background** - Horticulture experience, years in space (expertise)
- [ ] **Brand mission** - What FF aims to do for readers (purpose)
- [ ] **Track record** - Site age, community size, articles published (authority)
- [ ] **Contact information** - Email, location, or contact form (trust)
- [ ] **Unique differentiator** - What makes FF different from competitors (positioning)
- [ ] **Honesty about failures** - FF kills plants too, honest approach (trust)
- [ ] **Testimonials or social proof** - Reader success stories (authority)
- [ ] **Affiliate disclosure** - Transparency about monetization (trust)
- [ ] **Update dates** - Show content is maintained (freshness)

## 3. Brand Voice Analysis

### 3.1 FF's Established Voice (from BRAND-BLUEPRINT.md)

**Tone:** Practical, slightly snarky, helpful without being preachy

**Key Characteristics:**
- Direct and honest (no sugarcoating)
- Admits when things are hard or when common advice is wrong
- Practical over perfect
- Witty without being mean
- Assumes reader has killed plants before

**Example Voice Lines:**
- "Overwatering kills more plants than underwatering. It's not even close."
- "Pebble trays don't work. Here's what actually increases humidity."
- "You've killed everything you've ever owned. These might survive you."

**What NOT To Do:**
- Be preachy or condescending
- Overpromise (plants die, that's okay)
- Use generic plant care jargon without explanation
- Pretend there's one right way

**What TO Do:**
- Explain why, not just what
- Acknowledge failure is normal
- Provide actionable, specific advice
- Link to related guides naturally

### 3.2 Voice Patterns from Existing Articles

Scanning existing FF articles reveals consistent voice patterns:

1. **Confident Directness:** "The ZZ is what happens when a plant decides to stop needing you." — No hedging, strong opinions.

2. **Self-Deprecating Humor:** "The honest guide to plants that won't judge you for forgetting to water them for three weeks."

3. **Practical Honesty:** "First, a confession: The phrase 'you just have to water it when you remember' is not a care instruction."

4. **Expertise Without Elitism:** Explains complex concepts (root rot, humidity) without talking down to readers.

5. **Triage Mindset:** Focuses on fixing problems, not judging readers for causing them.

### 3.3 Brand Voice Summary for About Page

The About page should maintain FF's signature voice while introducing the brand. This means:

- **Honest, not corporate** — No empty promises like "we'll make you a plant expert in  week!"
- **Witty but warm** — Self-aware humor about plant killing normalizes failure
- **Practical over aspirational** — Focus on what actually works vs. aesthetic fantasies
- **Expert but accessible** — Knowledgeable without gatekeeping or jargon
- **Direct about monetization** — Affiliate disclosure, no pretending otherwise

## 4. Recommendations for FF About Page

### 4.1 Recommended Structure

Based on competitor analysis and FF brand voice, here's the recommended About page structure:

#### Section 1: The Hook (Who We Are)
- One-line positioning: "Plant care for people who've killed everything they've ever owned"
- Or: "The honest houseplant guide for people who are bad at plants"

#### Section 2: The Origin Story
- When FF started and why
- Founder's personal plant journey (failures included)
- What prompted creating FF

#### Section 3: Our Philosophy
- The honest approach: we kill plants too
- Why FF exists: demystify plant care without the gatekeeping
- What makes FF different: practical advice, no fluff, direct about what works

#### Section 4: What We Offer
- Number of articles/guides
- What readers can find (care guides, troubleshooting, recommendations)
- Affiliate disclosure here

#### Section 5: Trust Signals (Optional)
- Contact information
- Social proof (if available)
- Site history/updates

### 4.2 Voice Guidelines for Each Section

| Section | Tone | Example Approach |
|---------|------|------------------|
| Hook | Punchy, self-aware | "We kill plants too. Here's what we've learned." |
| Origin | Personal, honest | Share real failures, not just successes |
| Philosophy | Direct, anti-gatekeep | "No 'just water it when you remember' nonsense" |
| Offer | Helpful, specific | "75+ guides from watering to pests" |
| Trust | Transparent | Contact info, disclosure, update dates |

### 4.3 Content That Would Differentiate FF

1. **The Failure Story** — Most competitors don't admit failure. FF should lead with it: "I killed my first twelve houseplants before I figured out why."

2. **Anti-Expertise Positioning** — Instead of "we're plant experts," position as "we learned the hard way so you don't have to."

3. **Specific Metrics** — Number of plants killed, number of articles written, years of experience (both successful and failed).

4. **Voice Consistency** — The About page should sound exactly like every other FF article. If someone reads the About page and then clicks to an article, the voice should feel continuous.

### 4.4 SEO Considerations

For the About page to rank well:

1. **Target Keywords:** "about houseplants," "plant care guides," "houseplant advice"
2. **Internal Links:** Link to core guides (watering guide, starter kit, plant er)
3. **Fresh Content:** Update with current year, site milestones
4. **Schema Markup:** Consider adding Organization or Person schema
