## Quick Identification Chart

<img src="/images/pest-size-comparison.png" alt="Pest size comparison chart" style="width:100%;border-radius:10px;margin:16px 0;background:#fff">

| Pest | Size | Look for | Where they hide |
|------|------|----------|-----------------|
| Fungus gnats | 2-3mm | Small black flies near soil | Soil surface |
| Spider mites | Microscopic | Fine webbing, stippled leaves | Leaf undersides |
| Mealybugs | 2-4mm | White cottony masses | Leaf joints, stems |
| Aphids | 1-3mm | Clusters of soft-bodied bugs | New growth |
| Scale | 2-5mm | Brown/tan bumps on stems | Stems, leaf veins |
| Thrips | 1-2mm | Silvery streaks on leaves | Inside flowers |

## Fungus Gnats

<span class="threat yellow">🟡 Annoying but usually not fatal</span>

<img src="/images/fungus-gnat-lifecycle.png" alt="Fungus gnat lifecycle diagram" style="width:100%;max-width:500px;border-radius:10px;margin:16px 0;background:#fff">

### What they look like
- Tiny black flies (~2mm) hovering around soil
- Larvae are white/translucent worms in soil

## Spider Mites

<span class="threat red">🔴 Can kill plants fast</span>

<img src="/images/spider-mite-damage.png" alt="Spider mite damage progression" style="width:100%;border-radius:10px;margin:16px 0;background:#fff">

### What they look like
- Microscopic (need a loupe to see clearly)
- Fine webbing on leaf undersides
- Leaves look dusty/stippled, then yellow and drop

### Treatment ladder
1. **Isolate immediately** — they spread fast
2. **Shower the plant** — knock them off with water
3. **Increase humidity** around affected plants
4. **Insecticidal soap** — apply every 3-5 days for 2-3 weeks

## Mealybugs

<span class="threat orange">🟠 Persistent and gross</span>

<img src="/images/mealybugs-illustration.png" alt="Mealybugs on plant stem" style="width:100%;max-width:400px;border-radius:10px;margin:16px 0">

### What they look like
- White, cottony masses in leaf joints
- Slow-moving, soft-bodied bugs under the fluff
- Can also infest roots

1. **Isolate the plant**

## Aphids

<span class="threat yellow">🟡 Common and manageable</span>

### What they look like
- Small, soft-bodied bugs in clusters
- Colors: green, black, white, or pink
- Often on new growth and flower buds
- Leave sticky "honeydew" residue

### Treatment ladder
1. **Blast with water** — knock them off
2. **Insecticidal soap** — 2-3 applications, 5 days apart  
   <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B000BQL8UY?tag=feralfoliage-20" class="product-link" target="_blank" rel="nofollow noopener">Safer Brand Insecticidal Soap →</a>
3. **Neem oil** — preventive/ongoing

## Scale

<span class="threat orange">🟠 Sneaky and hard to spot</span>

### What they look like
- Brown/tan bumps that don't move
- Waxy or armored shell
- Often on stems and leaf veins

### Treatment ladder
1. **Manual removal** — scrape off with fingernail
2. **Alcohol swab** — wipe down affected areas
3. **Horticultural oil** — smothers them
4. **Systemic granules** — for heavy infestations

## Thrips

<span class="threat orange">🟠 Small but destructive</span>

### What they look like
- Tiny (1-2mm), slender insects
- Silver/bronze streaks on leaves
- Black dots (frass) on leaves

### Treatment ladder
1. **Remove affected leaves/flowers**
2. **Blue sticky traps** (thrips prefer blue)
3. **Insecticidal soap** — thorough coverage, repeat

4. **Systemic granules** — for persistent infestations

## Prevention Tips

- **Quarantine new plants** for 2-3 weeks
- **Inspect regularly** — check leaf undersides weekly
- **Keep humidity appropriate** — spider mites love dry air
- **Don't overwater** — fungus gnats breed in wet soil
- **Clean your tools** — don't spread pests
