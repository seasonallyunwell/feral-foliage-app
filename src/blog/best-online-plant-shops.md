## Etsy
**What They're Known For:** Unique, individual plants and rare cuttings often sold by hobbyist growers. Great for finding variegated varieties or specific props you can't find elsewhere.

**Price Range:** $15 – $150+

**Shipping Notes:** Shipping costs vary wildly by seller. Some offer combined shipping for multiple plants. Since sellers are individuals, communication is key—ask about packaging before buying.

---

## Amazon
**What They're Known For:** Convenience, fast shipping (Prime), and bulk supplies. Plant selection includes popular basics like Pothos, Snake Plants, and Lucky Bamboo, plus seeds and gardening tools.

**Price Range:** $10 – $50

**Shipping Notes:** Fast and reliable with Prime. Plants often ship quickly but can sometimes arrive stressed. Look for "Amazon's Choice" items for better reliability.

---

## Tropical Collectors
**What They're Known For:** Rare Aroids and collector-grade plants. They are known for healthy, well-established specimens and high-quality packaging.

**Price Range:** $40 – $300+

**Shipping Notes:** Specialized packaging ensures plants arrive in great condition. Shipping costs are calculated at checkout. They often have seasonal drops.

---

## Steve's Leaves
**What They're Known For:** Rare and exotic houseplants with a focus on aroids and variegated forms. Known for excellent customer service and healthy plants.

**Price Range:** $30 – $500+

**Shipping Notes:** Domestic US shipping. Careful packaging is their hallmark. They often restock weekly, so timing your visit is crucial.

---

## Palm Street
**What They're Known For:** Architectural plants and large statement pieces. They specialize in Bananas, Palms, and other large-leafed tropicals that are hard to find locally.

**Price Range:** $50 – $500+

**Shipping Notes:** Ships large specimens, which requires careful handling. Delivery times can be longer due to plant size. They offer freight shipping for the biggest plants.

---

## NSE (Nursery Source Expeditions)
**What They're Known For:** A vast selection of plants directly sourced from growers. They act almost like a plant wholesaler open to the public, offering competitive prices on volume.

**Price Range:** $15 – $100+

**Shipping Notes:** Shipping is calculated based on weight and size. They often have sales on bulk orders.

---

## Cardi Plants
**What They're Known For:** Stylish, Instagram-worthy plants paired with designer pots. They focus on aesthetics, making it easy to buy a plant that looks good immediately.

**Price Range:** $40 – $200+

**Shipping Notes:** Fast shipping and beautiful packaging. Their "Planter + Plant" combos are ready-to-display upon arrival.

---

## Final Thoughts

Buying plants online has never been easier, but choosing the right shop depends on what you're looking for. Whether you're hunting for rare collector items or just need a low-maintenance Snake Plant for your desk, these shops have you covered.
