## Under $25

Great for stocking stuffers, white elephant gifts, or a sweet addition to a larger present.

### 1. Propagation Station
A simple glass vessel or DIY kit for rooting water props. It's the gateway drug to the plant hobby.

### 2. Plant Mister
A stylish brass or ceramic mister adds a touch of class to plant care.

### 3. Succulent/Cactus Mix
High-quality soil is often overlooked but crucial for healthy roots.

### 4. Plant Labels & Markers
Cute, waterproof markers help track what you have.

## Under $100

Premium gifts that become permanent fixtures in their home.

### 1. Stylish Watering Can
A beautiful stainless steel or matte black watering can becomes a piece of decor.

### 2. Decorative Planter (Ceramic/Handmade)
Skip the cheap plastic nursery pots. A handmade ceramic pot is a game-changer.

### 3. Digital Hygrometer/Thermometer
Monitor the climate in their plant room to prevent humidity-related issues.

### 4. Subscription Box (Single Month)
Try a service like *Sill* or *Bloomscape* for a curated box of plants and goods.

## $100+

The "I really, really like you" or "Major Milestone" gift.

### 1. Floor Grow Light
A full-spectrum LED panel on a stand. Allows them to grow plants in dark corners.

### 2. Rare Plant Cutting
A specific variegated Monstera or Philodendron sold on Etsy or specialized sites.

### 3. Indoor Greenhouse Cabinet
A glass cabinet (like an IKEA cabinet hack or a dedicated greenhouse) for high-humidity plants.

### 4. Membership to a Botanical Garden
Or a private nursery tour/experience.

---

## Final Tips for Gifting Plants

1. **Ask about their light conditions** before buying a light-dependent plant.
2. **Consider their pet safety.** If they have cats/dogs, stick to pet-safe plants like Palms or Ferns.
3. **Include care instructions.** Even better, offer to help them pot it up!
