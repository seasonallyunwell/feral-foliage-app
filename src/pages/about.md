# About Feral Foliage

<p style="margin:0 0 16px 0;color:#a7a7a7">Plant care advice generated by AI, tested by real people.</p>

---

## The Short Version

This site is written by AI. All of it. Every guide, every recommendation, every troubleshooting article — generated, not penned by a horticulturist with dirt under their fingernails.

But it's not *just* AI. There's a human in the loop — she sets the direction, shapes the strategy, reviews every guide before it goes live, and makes sure we don't publish nonsense. Think of it as AI doing the writing, a human doing the editing.

We figure that's worth saying up front.

Here's why it still works: we've absorbed thousands of plant care resources — forums where people ask real questions, Reddit threads where people admit what killed their plants, extension articles, botanical garden guides, and product reviews from people who actually bought the thing. We identified what actually conflicts versus what gets mindlessly repeated, then synthesized it into practical advice.

The "failures" in our guides are real failures — aggregated from real forums where real people killed real plants. We researched what went wrong, found what actually fixed it, and documented it. Not made up. Collected.

---

## Why This Exists

We wanted a place with practical plant advice — no fluff, no gatekeeping, no "just water it" without explaining how. A site that treats you like a smart person who happens to not know this one thing, rather than someone too stupid to keep a pothos alive.

We built this to be the resource we wanted when we were killing plants. Useful, honest, and updated when we learn something new.

## What We Offer

- **Troubleshooting guides** — Something dying? We cover [brown tips](/blog/brown-tips/), [root rot](/blog/treat-root-rot/), [pests](/blog/pest-id/), and basically every way a plant can look unhappy.

- **Plant-specific care guides** — From [alocasias](/blog/alocasia-dying/) to [pothos](/blog/pothos-complete-care-guide/), we break down what each plant actually needs without the fluff.

- **Tools & calculators** — Watering calculators, light meters, soil mixers. Things that help instead of just looking nice on a shelf.

- **Gear recommendations** — We link to products we'd actually use. If we don't trust it, we don't recommend it.

## The Honest Pitch

AI writes the guides. A human reviews every one before publication, sets the direction, and catches the things we get wrong. We don't publish anything we haven't both signed off on.

The advice here is built from real experiences — just not ours. We read what killed other people's plants, found the solutions, and wrote guides so you don't have to make the same mistakes.

**Contact us:** A human reads emails. Questions, corrections, suggestions — we'll update the site accordingly.
